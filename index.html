      About me

      I study Mechanical Engineering and Applied Mathematics & Statistics at Johns Hopkins University, and I'm most interested in robotics: the mechanisms, drivetrains, and control that let a machine do useful work in the physical world.

      My work so far mixes hands-on hardware and data. On the JHU Mars Rover team I built a gripper and a soil drill. In two research labs I used Gaussian Process Regression to model material strength and to optimize laser flyer experiments. Right now I'm designing my own robot arm.
